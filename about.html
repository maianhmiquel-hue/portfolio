Work About

Alice Miquel is a designer and researcher based in Paris. Her work examines our relationships with objects, and more particularly with technological ones, from a critical and research-led perspective. Rooted in the tradition of critical and speculative design, her practice approaches design as a means of questioning the assumptions carried by industrial production and technological innovation, rather than as a way of simply adding new products to the world. This inquiry takes shape in design projects that bring together textiles, biomaterials and living organisms with digital and robotic technologies. The same questions inform her work within companies, where she is involved in the management of technology and innovation, bringing a critical perspective to the way technologies are designed, developed and used, with the aim of helping, across design and management, to make other technological futures thinkable.

Alongside her design and research work, she is actively involved in the field of contemporary art, with a particular interest in emerging art and design practices. Her engagement with this scene extends the questions that run through her own work, from the place of technology in our lives to the agency of materials and the shifting boundary between the living and the manufactured. She writes articles on exhibitions and artists whose work engages with these issues, and works as a curator, creating spaces where emerging practices can be discovered and set in dialogue, across the boundaries that usually separate art, design and research.

Alice Miquel trained in object design at ENSAAMA in Paris. She went on to complete a double Master's degree in Fashion & Materials and in Research in Management between the École des Arts Décoratifs-PSL and Université Paris Dauphine-PSL. She is currently pursuing a Master's degree in Management of Innovation at École Polytechnique.
